### 📊 Dashboard Features & Pages

1. **Overview (Financial Performance & Market Share):**  
   Company Market Share (Uber vs. Lyft), Net Revenue Share, Monthly Revenue Trends, and Revenue-to-Margin Bridge Waterfall Analysis.

2. **Operations (Spatial Demand & Operational Dynamics):**  
   Top Active Pick-up Zones, Service Zone Distribution, Borough Demand Patterns, and Dual-Axis Demand & Wait Time Analysis across time windows.

3. **Economics (Pricing & Driver Pay Behavior):**  
   Driver Pay Share %, Hourly Congestion Fee Revenue, Trip Distance vs. Fare Scatter Plot, and Weekday vs. Weekend Earnings Comparison.
